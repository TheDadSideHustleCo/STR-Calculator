How to Use This Calculator
A quick guide to getting accurate, actionable results in under 5 minutes.

1 Follow the Tabs in Order

🏠
Tab 1 — Property Info
Enter purchase price, down payment %, closing cost %, comparable market rate, and long-term rental estimate. These anchor all your returns.
💰
Tab 2 — Revenue & Rates
Set your nightly rate, cleaning fee, average stay length, and platform fee. Use the seasonal grid to adjust occupancy and rate multipliers month-by-month, or leave defaults for a flat estimate.
📋
Tab 3 — Mortgage & Expenses
Use the mortgage calculator, then click "Use This Amount →" to auto-fill your monthly payment. Fill in actual expenses or use the pre-filled defaults as a starting point.
📊
Tab 4 — Results & Analysis
Your full analysis updates in real time as you change inputs. Save two scenarios to compare side-by-side on Tab 5.

2 Understanding Your Results

Annual Gross Revenue
Total income before fees and expenses — room nights + cleaning fees collected.
Annual Net Profit
What you keep after all costs. Divide by 12 for monthly cash flow.
Profit Margin
Net ÷ gross revenue. Many investors look for 25–30%+ (rule of thumb).
Cap Rate
Net Operating Income ÷ Purchase Price. NOI is income after all operating costs, before the mortgage. Independent of financing. 6–8%+ is strong.
Cash-on-Cash Return
Net profit ÷ cash invested (down + closing). Many investors target 8–12%+ (rule of thumb).
ADR
Average Daily Rate — room revenue per booked night, excluding cleaning fees.
RevPAR
ADR × Occupancy %. A widely used STR performance metric.
Breakeven Occupancy
Minimum occupancy to cover all costs. If your actual occupancy exceeds this, you're profitable.
Payback Period
Years until cumulative profit equals your cash invested (excluding appreciation).
Depreciation Benefit
Estimated annual tax deduction from building depreciation (39 or 27.5 years, your choice). Consult a CPA.

3 Investment Grade Explained

A
Strong Investment
Cap rate ≥ 8%, CoC ≥ 12%, breakeven < 50%. All three tests passed.
B
Solid Investment
Cap rate ≥ 5%, CoC ≥ 8%, breakeven < 60%. Good returns with room to optimize.
C
Marginal Investment
Cap rate ≥ 3%, CoC ≥ 4%, breakeven < 75%. Below benchmarks — consider raising rate or cutting expenses.
D
Review Your Numbers
Below all benchmarks. Re-evaluate pricing strategy, expenses, or the property itself.

4 Saving & Loading Your Work

💾
Save to File
Click 💾 Save in the top-right header. A .json file named after your property (e.g. Beachfront-Bungalow-STR-Analysis.json) will download to your computer. This file contains all your inputs and any saved scenarios.
📂
Load from File
Click 📂 Load in the header and choose a previously saved .json file. All inputs and scenarios will be restored exactly as you left them. You can keep a separate save file for each property you're analysing.
🔄
Quick Resume (same browser)
The calculator also mirrors your last save to browser storage, so if you close and reopen the file in the same browser without clearing history, your last session loads automatically — no file needed.

5 Pro Tips

Mortgage flow: Always click "Use This Amount →" after adjusting the mortgage calculator. Otherwise the expense field stays at its previous value.
Expense units: Fields labeled /yr (Taxes, Insurance, License, Furniture, CPA) are entered annually — auto-divided by 12. All other dollar fields are monthly.
Seasonal rates: The Rate × slider multiplies your base nightly rate. 1.5 = 150% of your base rate. Below 1.0 = a discount. Occupancy sliders set each month independently.
Scenario Compare: Save Scenario A (base case) → adjust inputs → Save Scenario B → go to Tab 5 for side-by-side comparison.
One file per property: Name your property clearly in Tab 1 before saving — the save file is named after it. This makes it easy to keep separate saves for multiple properties.
Depreciation is not cash: It's a paper deduction that reduces taxable income. Your actual cash profit is the Net Profit figure. Consult a CPA for your specific situation.
